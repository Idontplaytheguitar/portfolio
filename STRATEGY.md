# Agus — Client Acquisition Playbook

You're at zero (no clients, no niche, no audience). Good — this is a clean start with one
unfair advantage: **you already have a flagship most devs could never build (Scale Lab).**
This is the plan to turn that into paying clients. Honest, concrete, no fluff.

---

## 1. The positioning (why this niche)

**You are the "interactive web experiences" specialist** — interactive data-viz, explorable
explainers, live product demos, real-time simulations, creative front-end.

Why this and not "web developer":
- **Differentiated.** 95% of freelancers build the same WordPress/Shopify/landing sites and
  compete on price. Almost nobody can do polished interactive/animated/real-time work.
- **Higher fees.** "Make me a website" = $500. "Build an interactive product demo that makes
  visitors convert" = $3–8k. Same effort, 10× the price, because it's rare and tied to value.
- **You already have proof.** Scale Lab. Pointing it at a prospect ends the "can you actually
  do this?" conversation instantly.
- **Reachable by cold outreach** — you don't need an audience; you need to email the right 200
  companies.

> If outbound to companies feels too slow at first, the *exact same positioning* wins on
> Upwork/Contra too, where "interactive/data-viz specialist" stands out from 10,000 generic devs.

## 2. The offer (what you sell)

Productized, fixed-fee. Fixed scope kills the scary "how much will this cost?" objection.

| Package | What it is | Starter price* | "Real" price |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Explainer** | Interactive explorable explanation of a product/concept/data | $1.5k | $4–6k |
| **The Demo** | Live interactive product demo / sandbox for a landing page | $2k | $5–8k |
| **The Dashboard** | Custom real-time data-viz or internal tool | $2.5k | $6–10k |
| **The Build** | Scoped bespoke interactive web app | from $3k | $8k+ |

\* **Starter price = your first 1–3 clients only.** Charge cheap to buy proof + testimonials.
The moment you have 2 case studies + 2 testimonials, jump to "real" prices. Underpricing past
client #3 is a trap, not humility.

**Fastest path to first dollar:** offer your first client a "founder rate" in exchange for a
testimonial + permission to use it as a case study. Frame it as a deal, not desperation.

## 3. Where the clients are (targets)

You're emailing companies that (a) have money, (b) have something complex to explain, (c) live
or die by their landing page / product clarity. Best fits:

1. **Seed/Series-A SaaS startups** — they need their product to *click* in 5 seconds. An
   interactive demo on the homepage is a direct conversion lever. (Find on: ProductHunt,
   YC company lists, Indie Hackers, "launched" posts on X/LinkedIn.)
2. **Dev-tools / API / data companies** — naturally love interactive docs, playgrounds, viz.
3. **Course creators / edtech / "explainer" people** — explorable explanations are catnip.
4. **Agencies & studios** — subcontract you for the hard interactive piece they can't staff.
   (One agency relationship = recurring work without you doing sales.)
5. **Newsletters / media with data** — interactive charts, scrollytelling pieces.

**Build a list of 100–200** in a spreadsheet: company, person, email, the specific thing you'd
build for *them*, status. (I can build you a lightweight CRM/tracker page if you want.)

## 4. The cold outreach (the engine)

Volume + specificity. 10–20 personalized emails/day. The template that works isn't "hi I'm a
dev" — it's "I noticed X about *you*, here's a thing I'd build, here's proof."

```
Subject: an interactive demo for [Company]?

Hi [Name],

I was looking at [Company] and [specific, true observation — e.g. "your homepage explains the
product with a static screenshot"]. For a tool like yours, letting visitors *play* with a live
demo right there usually converts way better than a video or screenshot.

I build exactly that — interactive product demos and explorable explainers. Here's a physics
lab I built from scratch so you can see the level: [SCALE_LAB_URL] and more at [PORTFOLIO_URL].

If useful, I'll mock up a 30-second concept of what an interactive demo for [Company] could look
like — no charge, no obligation. Worth a quick look?

— Agus
```

Rules:
- **One specific observation about THEM** per email. No mass blasts.
- **Lead with proof** (Scale Lab link). It does the convincing.
- **Tiny ask** ("worth a look?"), not "hire me."
- **Follow up twice**, 3–4 days apart. Most replies come from follow-ups.
- Track everything. Sent → opened → replied → call → proposal → won.

Warm channels to run in parallel: Upwork/Contra (apply to interactive/data-viz/animation jobs
with the same positioning), X/Twitter + Indie Hackers (post your builds — Scale Lab is a banger
"I built this" post), relevant Discords/Slacks.

## 6. The product question (later, not now)

You don't have a product idea because you don't have a niche yet — and that's backwards-correct.
**Do the client work first.** Client work hands you the product idea for free: after 3–5 projects
you'll see the same painful thing requested over and over → that repeated thing is your micro-SaaS.
Build the product *out of* a validated client pain, not out of a guess. Don't force it now.
